## Get to know us

[Composable Security 🛡️⛓️](https://twitter.com/Composable_Sec) performs **threat-based security reviews that go beyond code**, across **Smart Contracts, Off-chain Components and AI Integrations**. We review smart contracts, custom blockchain implementations, oracles, bridges, relayers and backend services, looking at how the whole system works and where its assumptions can break.

We are actively expanding our work in **AI security**, researching emerging threats and reviewing AI agents, LLM-powered applications, AI workflows and development environments. Our focus includes prompt injection, unsafe tool access, data exposure and the risks that arise when AI can act on real systems.

Since 2022, we have repeatedly worked at the frontier of new technology, taking on security challenges where established practices and specialist expertise were still limited. From custom blockchain implementations to complex AI integrations and AI agents, we combine this hands-on experience with the knowledge gained during our earlier **6+ years securing global fintechs and Polish banks**.

**Trusted by market leaders including Mistral AI, Lido, RedStone, Tellor and Filecoin**, alongside YieldNest, Othentic and many more.

Learn more [about us](https://composable-security.com/about/) and [our work](https://composable-security.com/our-work/).
